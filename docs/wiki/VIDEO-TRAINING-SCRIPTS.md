# Training Video Scripts & Production Guide
## 3 Boxes Luxury Curations Platform

This document contains detailed video scripts for recording training videos with voiceover, organized by user role and functionality.

---

## 🎬 Video Production Setup

### Recording Tools
- **Screen Recording**: OBS Studio / Loom / Camtasia
- **Voice Recording**: Audacity / Built-in mic with noise cancellation
- **Video Editing**: DaVinci Resolve / Adobe Premiere
- **Format**: MP4, 1920x1080, 30fps
- **Audio**: Clear voiceover, 48kHz, noise-removed

### Standard Intro (All Videos)
> "Welcome to 3 Boxes Luxury Training. In this video, we'll walk through [TOPIC]. Let's get started."

### Standard Outro (All Videos)
> "That covers [TOPIC]. If you have questions, contact support at info@3boxes.in. Thank you for watching!"

## 📹 ADMIN TRAINING VIDEOS

### Video 1: Admin Dashboard Overview (8 min)
**Target**: Admin | **Category**: onboarding

**[0:00-0:30]** Intro - Show login page
> "Welcome to the 3 Boxes Luxury Admin Dashboard. This is your command center for managing the entire platform. Let me walk you through every section."

**[0:30-2:00]** Login & 2FA
> "First, log in with your admin credentials at the sign-in page. After entering your password, you'll be prompted for 2-factor authentication. Enter the OTP sent to your email or use your authenticator app. Once verified, you'll see the admin dashboard."

**[2:00-3:30]** Dashboard Overview
> "The dashboard shows key metrics: total products, orders, revenue, and user counts. You'll see recent orders, top products, and sales charts. The sidebar on the left gives you access to all admin functions."

**[3:30-5:00]** Sidebar Navigation
> "Let's go through each sidebar item: Products for managing inventory, Categories for organizing products, Inventory for stock levels, Orders for processing, Users & Perms for managing access, Content for wiki docs, and the new Training & Wiki section for managing training content."

**[5:00-6:30]** Quick Actions
> "From any page, you can use the search bar to find products, orders, or users quickly. The top-right shows your profile and the sign-out button."

**[6:30-8:00]** Theme & Settings
> "Toggle between light and dark themes using the sun/moon icon. For SMTP and email settings, navigate to the Settings section. Always save changes before navigating away."

### Video 2: Product Management (10 min)
**Target**: Admin | **Category**: feature-tutorial

**[0:00-0:30]** Intro
> "Products are the heart of your store. Let's learn how to add, edit, and manage products effectively."

**[0:30-3:00]** Adding a New Product
> "Click 'Products' in the sidebar, then click 'Add Product'. Fill in the product name, description, and price. Set the compare-at price for discounts. Select the category and add relevant tags. Upload at least 3 product images for best results — the first image is the primary display image."

**[3:00-5:00]** Product Variants
> "If your product comes in different sizes or colors, add variants. Click 'Add Variant', specify the variant name like 'Size' or 'Color', and list the options. Each variant can have its own price adjustment and stock level."

**[5:00-7:00]** External Products
> "3 Boxes Luxury also supports external products from partner platforms like Myntra, Amazon, and Nykaa. When adding an external product, toggle 'External Product', select the platform, and add the affiliate URL. These products show 'Shop on Platform' instead of 'Add to Cart'."

**[7:00-8:30]** Inventory Management
> "After creating products, manage stock levels in the Inventory tab. Set stock quantities, low-stock alerts, and track inventory movements. Products with zero stock are automatically marked as out of stock."

**[8:30-10:00]** Bulk Operations
> "Use the Import tab to bulk-import products from CSV files or from Shopify. The system automatically maps categories and handles duplicate detection."

---

### Video 3: Order Processing & Fulfillment (8 min)
**Target**: Admin | **Category**: feature-tutorial

**[0:00-0:30]** Intro
> "Processing orders efficiently ensures customer satisfaction. Let's walk through the complete order lifecycle."

**[0:30-2:30]** Viewing Orders
> "Navigate to the Orders tab. You'll see all orders with their status, customer info, and total amount. Use filters to view orders by status: pending, confirmed, shipped, delivered, or cancelled."

**[2:30-4:30]** Processing an Order
> "Click on an order to view details. Here you'll see the customer's shipping address, items ordered, and payment status. To process: first confirm the order, then update inventory, pack the items, and mark as shipped. Add tracking information so customers can follow their delivery."

**[4:30-6:00]** Handling Returns & Refunds
> "If a customer requests a return, navigate to the order and click 'Process Refund'. Enter the refund amount and reason. The system supports partial refunds. Refunds are processed through the original payment method."

**[6:00-8:00]** Order Reports
> "The Reports tab shows order analytics: daily/weekly/monthly sales, average order value, and popular products. Export reports as CSV for accounting."

---

### Video 4: Training Content Management (7 min)
**Target**: Admin | **Category**: admin-training

**[0:00-0:30]** Intro
> "The Training & Wiki section lets you manage all documentation, SOPs, training videos, and patent documents — and control who can access them."

**[0:30-2:30]** Documents Sub-tab
> "The Documents tab shows all wiki content. Click 'New Document' to create one. Enter the title — the slug auto-generates. Select the category and document type. For access control, check which roles can view this document. You can also load pre-built documentation templates using the 'Load from File' button."

**[2:30-4:30]** Training Videos Sub-tab
> "The Training Videos tab manages video content. Add a video by entering its title, YouTube or Vimeo embed URL, duration, and target audience. Videos are stored as special wiki documents with docType 'video'. Users with matching roles will see these in their wiki section."

**[4:30-6:00]** Share Content Sub-tab
> "Sharing lets you control who sees what. Select a document, then choose roles or specific users. Set whether they can view or download. This is especially useful for sensitive content like patent documents or internal SOPs."

**[6:00-7:00]** Patent Docs Sub-tab
> "The Patent Docs section provides special handling for patent documentation. Track filing status, patent numbers, and important dates. Patent documents are only visible to admins by default — share with legal team using the Share Content tab."

### Video 6: AI Virtual Try-On (6 min)
**Target**: User | **Category**: feature-tutorial

**[0:00-0:30]** Intro
> "One of our most exciting features is the AI Virtual Try-On. See how products look on you before buying!"

**[0:30-2:30]** Starting a Try-On
> "Navigate to any product and scroll down to the 'Virtual Try-On' section. Click 'Try On This Product'. You'll be prompted to upload a photo of yourself. For best results, use a well-lit, front-facing photo with a clear background."

**[2:30-4:00]** AI Processing
> "After uploading, our AI processes your image. This takes about 10-30 seconds. The AI maps your body proportions and overlays the product realistically. You'll see the result with a watermark."

**[4:00-5:00]** Sharing & Gallery
> "Like your try-on result? Share it to the AI Style Gallery! Other shoppers can see and like your style. Your privacy is protected — only your first name initial is shown. You can share up to 3 images per product."

**[5:00-6:00]** Image Pop-Out
> "Click on any image in the gallery to see it full-size in the lightbox. Use the zoom button to see details. You can also like images directly from the lightbox view."

### Video 8: 3Box Curate - Cross-Portal Shopping (7 min)
**Target**: User | **Category**: feature-tutorial

**[0:00-0:30]** Intro
> "3Box Curate lets you shop from multiple portals — Myntra, Amazon, Nykaa, and more — all in one place, delivered as a single package!"

**[0:30-2:30]** Browsing Products
> "Navigate to '3Box Curate' from the top navigation. Browse products from 9 different platforms. Use the platform tabs to filter by your favorite portal. Each product shows the platform badge, name, and price."

**[2:30-4:30]** Building Your Bundle
> "Click the '+' button on any product to add it to your bundle. The 'Your Bundle' sidebar shows all added items. Important: your bundle must include at least one 3Box product. Get 5% off for 3+ items and 10% off for 5+ items!"

**[4:30-6:00]** AI Curation & Consent
> "When ready, click 'Proceed with AI Curation'. Read the consent form carefully — you're authorizing 3Box to purchase on your behalf from other portals. Check all three consent boxes and confirm. Your bundle will be processed and delivered in 5-7 business days."

**[6:00-7:00]** Tracking Your Bundle
> "After confirmation, you'll receive a unique Bundle ID. Track your bundle from the Orders section. All items arrive in one package, saving you the hassle of tracking multiple deliveries."
